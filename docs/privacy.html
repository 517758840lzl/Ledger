Privacy Policy

Qingji keeps the book on this phone. There is no Qingji account and no server of ours. This page describes what this version actually stores and what it does with it.

What the book holds

  • Accounts: name, type, and opening balance. For a credit card, the opening balance is the amount owed. A stock or fund marked as manual changes only when you edit the balance, not when you add an entry.
  • Entries: expense, income, or transfer, with the amount, account, the other account on a transfer, category, subcategory, note, and date.
  • A monthly budget total, any category cap you set, and one savings goal.
  • Categories: the name, whether it is in use, its subcategories, and the Essential or Can cut mark.

Where it is kept

The book is in this app's own folder. Language, currency, and the reminder time stay on this phone and are not synced.

iCloud runs only when this phone is signed in and this app is allowed to use its own iCloud container. What syncs is the accounts, categories, entries, budgets, and savings goal, into that Apple ID's private iCloud. Another device on the same Apple ID can receive them. Qingji does not receive the Apple ID or the password, and cannot open that iCloud copy. Without the container, the book stays on this phone.

Reminder

The daily reminder is off until you turn it on and pick a time. The phone then shows one local notice: time to log today's spending. The notice has no amount, account, or note. Turning the reminder off cancels it. Declining notification permission does not affect the book.

Export, import, and clear

Export writes one CSV and hands it to the system share sheet. You choose where it goes. Qingji does not receive the file.

Importing a file exported by this app replaces the accounts, categories, entries, budgets, and savings goal on this phone. A plain transaction table only adds rows to the cash account and does not replace accounts. A file that cannot be read leaves the current book unchanged. Loading sample bills adds sample entries to the book you already have.

Clear removes entries, budgets, and the extra accounts, and leaves one cash account. Categories, language, currency, the reminder, and the savings goal stay. If iCloud sync is on, the cleared book syncs to the other devices on the same Apple ID.

Uninstalling deletes the book on this phone. Anything already in iCloud stays until you delete it there.

What it is used for

The book is used to show balances, net worth, the monthly report, and the budget. Those figures are calculated on the phone. Qingji does not connect to a bank or a payment account, does not read contacts, messages, photos, or location, and has no ads.
