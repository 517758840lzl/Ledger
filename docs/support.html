Help with Qingji

Qingji is a spending book on your iPhone. Record expenses, income, and transfers, then look at the month, the budget, and what you own. There is no Qingji account.

The app

How to use it

Write an entry

On Flow, tap +. Choose an expense, income, or a transfer between accounts. Add the amount, category, and a note if you want one.

Read the month

Insights shows this month's balance, how spending compares with last month, and the budget. You can set one monthly total and a cap for a category.

See what you own

Assets lists cash, cards, and accounts you update by hand. A credit card balance is the amount you owe. A stock or fund marked as manual changes only when you edit it.

Look back

Open a day on the calendar, or a past month in history. Search Flow by amount, category, or note.

Export and import

Export writes a CSV and hands it to the iPhone share sheet. You choose where it goes. Importing a Qingji export replaces the book on that iPhone. A plain transaction table only adds rows to cash.

Reminder, language, currency

The daily reminder stays off until you turn it on in Settings. The notice does not include an amount, account, or note. Language and currency switch inside the app.

Where the book is kept

The book is stored in the app's own folder on the iPhone. Language, currency, and the reminder time stay on that phone. iCloud runs only when the iPhone is signed in and Qingji is allowed to use its own iCloud container. Without that container, the book stays on the phone. Qingji does not connect to a bank, does not read contacts, messages, photos, or location, and does not show ads.

The full account of what is stored is in the privacy policy.
